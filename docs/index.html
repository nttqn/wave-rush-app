How it plays

One-touch control

Hold anywhere to fly up at 45°, release to dive. Floors and ceilings are safe to slide along. Only spikes, saws and the side of a wall will crash you.

12 levels + Endless

From Easy to Insane, with speed and mini portals. Endless mode builds a new course every run and gets harder the farther you fly.

Level editor

Place walls, spikes, saws and portals. Test-play your level, prove it's beatable with one tap, then share it with friends as a level code.

Instant retries

Crash and you're flying again in under a second, with an attempt counter, best % per level and wave skins to unlock.
