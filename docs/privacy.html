Privacy Policy for Wave Rush

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Wave Rush ("the app") is an arcade game for Android. This page explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with.

Information collected directly by the app

Wave Rush does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifying information. The only data the app stores itself is kept locally on your device (via Android's standard app storage) and is never transmitted anywhere by the app:

  • Your progress: best percentage and number of attempts for each level, and your best Endless-mode distance.
  • Your selected wave skin, and your music and sound-effect on/off preferences.
  • Levels you build in the level editor, and your best percentage on them.

This local data is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Sharing levels

When you share a level you built, the app copies a text "level code" to your device's clipboard. The code contains only the level's name and layout. You decide where to paste it. When you import a level, the app reads the clipboard only if you tap the paste button in the import dialog. The app has no online level service, and level codes are never uploaded anywhere by the app.

Advertising

Wave Rush shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep the game free. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), general device information, and approximate location to serve and measure ads. It may also use this data to personalize ads, depending on your device and account settings. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by Wave Rush directly. You can review or adjust how Google uses this data for ads through your device's ad settings. Learn more at Google's Ads Privacy & Terms.

Data sharing

Wave Rush does not sell your data. It does not share any data with third parties beyond Google AdMob, described above, which the advertising feature needs in order to work.

Children's privacy

Wave Rush is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising is provided by Google's SDK, which offers its own settings and controls for compliance with applicable children's privacy regulations.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy. Check back here for the latest version.
