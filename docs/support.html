Support for Wave Rush

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Wave Rush is a one-touch arcade game for Android. You steer a neon arrow through zig-zag tunnels, spikes and saws, and try to reach 100% on every level.

How to play

  • Hold anywhere on the screen to fly up at 45°. Release to fly down.
  • Floors and ceilings are safe: you can slide along them. Spikes, saws and the vertical side of a wall end the attempt.
  • Portals change your speed (0.8×–1.5×) or switch you to a smaller, steeper mini wave.
  • After a crash the level restarts instantly. Your best % for each level is saved.
  • Endless mode builds a new course every run and gets harder as you go.
  • Tap the pause button, or press the Android back button, to pause during a run.

Level editor

  • Open CREATE on the home screen, then tap NEW.
  • Pick a tool on the left and tap to place it. Drag sideways to scroll.
  • Tap ▶ to test-play. Tap ✓ to check that the level can be beaten. If it can't, a red line marks the impossible spot.
  • Verified levels can be shared: Share copies a level code, and friends add it with IMPORT.

Common questions

I don't hear any sound

Check the music and sound-effect buttons on the home screen or in the pause menu, and make sure your device isn't in silent mode.

My progress or my levels disappeared

Progress and levels you build are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. To keep a level safe, share it and save the level code somewhere.

Something's broken, or you have feedback

Please include your device model and Android version (for example "Redmi Note 13, Android 14") and describe what happened. It helps a lot in tracking issues down.
