Thank you for reviewing Mirrorwing.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE PREVIOUS SUBMISSION ("Wave Rush" 1.0.1, rejected under 4.3)
- New name and identity: Mirrorwing.
- New mechanics built around the name: DUAL portals add a mirrored twin arrow that the player controls at the same time (both must avoid every hazard), and FLIP portals reverse the controls. Four new levels (13-16) are designed around them.
- New Daily Challenge: one generated level per day, the same for every player, with a streak counter.
- Ghost replays: the player's best attempt on each level is replayed exactly beside them.
- Hints: after four crashes near the same spot, the game draws a path that is known to work there. This is possible because every level is generated around a guaranteed path.
- All in-game music and sound effects were replaced with audio created for this app only.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
No login, no account and no in-app purchases. Everything is available right after launch.

HOW TO TEST QUICKLY
- The game runs in landscape only (iPhone and iPad, full screen).
- Touch and hold anywhere: the arrow rises while you hold and falls when you release. Floors and ceilings are safe to slide along; spikes, saws and vertical wall edges end the attempt, and it restarts instantly.
- Level 14 ("Twin Signal") shows the twin arrow within its first ten seconds. Level 15 ("Flipside") has a FLIP portal about four seconds in. All levels are unlocked.
- DAILY on the home screen opens today's challenge. CREATE opens the level editor: tap NEW, choose a tool on the left and tap the grid; the check-mark button proves the level can be beaten.
- Pause with the button at the top right.

DATA AND ADS
Progress, settings, ghost recordings and player-made levels are stored only on the device. The app shows Google AdMob ads: a banner on menu screens and occasional interstitials between levels, never during gameplay. It does not use App Tracking Transparency and does not request the IDFA.
