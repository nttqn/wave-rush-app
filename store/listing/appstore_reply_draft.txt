Hello, and thank you for the earlier review. I took the 4.3 feedback seriously and rebuilt the app before resubmitting. It is now version 1.1.0 (build 3), renamed "Mirrorwing". Answers to your questions:

1. Mirrorwing is a one-touch reflex game. An arrow flies forward; holding the screen makes it climb and releasing makes it dive. Its central mechanic is the mirrored twin: after a DUAL portal the player steers two arrows at once, one the mirror image of the other, and both must avoid every hazard. FLIP portals reverse the controls. It offers short, skill-based sessions with instant retries.

2. Players who enjoy hard, reflex-based arcade games, plus players who like building and sharing their own levels. Teenagers and adults.

3. Three things set it apart from other games of this type. (a) The twin arrow: steering one input through two mirrored paths at once. (b) Every level is generated around a guaranteed path, so the game can always show a stuck player a route that works there, and the level editor can prove a player-made level is beatable before it is shared. (c) A daily challenge that is the same for everyone, and exact ghost replays of your own best run.

5. It is a standalone product, not part of a suite. No other app on my account has this gameplay.
