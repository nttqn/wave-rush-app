Welcome to Mirrorwing, the new name of Wave Rush, with a big update:

- Twin arrows: fly through a DUAL portal and a mirrored twin joins you. Both of you have to make it through.
- Flipped controls: FLIP portals swap your controls (hold to fall, release to rise).
- 4 new levels built around the new portals, now 16 in total.
- Daily Challenge: a new level every day, the same for every player, with a streak to keep.
- Race your ghost: your best attempt on each level replays beside you.
- Hints: stuck on one spot? After a few crashes, glowing dots show a path that works.
- All-new music and sound effects.
- Fixes and polish.
